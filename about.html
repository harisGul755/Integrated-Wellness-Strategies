About

Debra S. Trasati

PMHNP-BC · Psychiatric Nurse Practitioner

Debra holds a Bachelor of Science in Nursing from Salem State College and a Master of Science in Nursing from the University of South Florida, and is certified as a Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner.

With 15 years of experience supporting children, adolescents and adults with anxiety, depression, attention difficulties and life-stage stressors, she has worked in community mental health, private practice and inpatient settings.
